Check My Job Fit

How this works: a simple keyword-and-rules check that runs on your phone. It is not AI and can miss things. Read every result and use your own judgement. It uses only what you type and never invents anything.

No CV yet? Type your details instead

This app cannot open links. Open the advert, press and hold the text, tap Select all, then Copy. Come back here and paste. If several jobs are listed, paste only the one you want. If the advert is a picture, type its requirements instead.

“Sample data” fills the form with a made-up person and a made-up advert, so you can see how results look.

Improve My CV

How this works: a rules-based check, not AI. It compares your CV with one advert and shows what is missing and what to put first. It never adds anything you did not write, and it does not rewrite your CV yet.

Word (.docx), PDF or text files. The file is read on your phone and sent nowhere. Or paste the text below.

Write My Cover Letter

How this works: a template letter, not AI. It uses only your CV and your own words, and you choose which strengths go in. Read every line and make sure it is true before you send it.
